BioComposer

Machine Learning at Berkeley × Google DeepMind · 12/2025 – present

Python framework that represents bioinformatics workflows as executable DAGs and derives tool execution order automatically. Tools are standardized with typed TOML manifests and a Rust-based container registry; Gemini-generated, inspectable Python connectors reconcile mismatched I/O formats and schemas.

Ships on PyPI (pip install biocomposer) with isolated Docker execution and local or Modal deployment. I served as product manager. Paper in draft.

iOSSwiftTime series

Geo Space Weather

iPhone & iPad · no accounts, no ads, no tracking

Shows the current state of Earth's magnetic environment on one screen. It reads live geomagnetic measurements from USGS observatories and planetary storm indices from NOAA and GFZ Potsdam, and runs ML models over 200+ years of sunspot records to project the solar cycle.

Every reading shows its source and when it was last updated. Forecasts are drawn separately from the historical record so an estimate is never mistaken for an observation.
